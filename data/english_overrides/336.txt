# 336 English as printed in the Korean-English hymnal, source: https://www.prayertents.com/hymns?nh=336
Faith of our fathers! living still In spite of dungeon,
fire and sword; O how our hearts beat high with joy
Whenever we hear God's glorious Word: Faith of our fathers,
holy faith! We will be true to Thee till death.

Our fathers, chained in prisons dark, Were still in heart and
conscience free; And blest would be their children's fate
If they, like them, should die for Thee: Faith of our fathers,
holy faith! We will be true to Thee till death.

Faith of our fathers! We will love Both friend and foe in
all our strife; And preach thee, too, as love knows how
By kindly words and virtuous life: Faith of our fathers,
holy faith! We will be true to Thee till death.
Amen.
