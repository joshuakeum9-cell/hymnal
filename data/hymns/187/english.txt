# 187 Come, Gracious Spirit, Heavenly Dove: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed (Korean-English hymnal wording)

Come, gracious Spirit, heavenly Dove,
With light and comfort from above:
Be Thou our guardian, Thou our guide!
Over every thought and step preside.

The light of truth to us display,
And make us know and choose Thy way;
Plant holy fear in every heart,
That we from God may never depart.

Lead us to holiness the road
Which we must take to dwell with God;
Lead us to Christ, the living way,
Nor let us from His presence stray.

Lead us to God, our final rest,
To be with Him forever blest;
Lead us to heaven, that we may share,
Fullness of joy forever there.
Amen.
