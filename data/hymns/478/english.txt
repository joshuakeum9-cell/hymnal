# 478 This is My Father's World: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed

God the Omnipotent! King, who ordainest
Thunder Thy clarion, the lightning Thy sword
Show forth Thy pity on high where Thou reignest
Give to us peace in our time, OLord.

God the Allmerciful! Earth hath forsaken
Meekness and mercy, and slighted Thy word
Let not Thy wrath in its terrors awaken
Give to us peace in our time, OLord.

God the Allrighteous One! Man hath defied Thee
Yet to eternity standeth Thy word
Falsehood and wrong shall not tarry beside Thee
Give to us peace in our time, OLord.

So shall Thy people, with thankful devotion,
Praise Him who saved them from peril and sword,
Singing in chorus from ocean to ocean,
Peace to the nations, and praise to the Lord. Amen.
