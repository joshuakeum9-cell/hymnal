# 104 O Come, O Come, Emmanuel: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed (Korean-English hymnal wording)

O come, O come, Emmanuel,
And ransom captive
Israel, That mourns in lonely exile here
Until the Son of God appear.
Rejoice! Rejoice! Emmanuel
Shall come to thee, O Israel!

O come, Thou Wisdom from on high,
And order all things
far and nigh; To us the path of knowledge show,
And cause us in her ways to go.
Rejoice! Rejoice! Emmanuel
Shall come to thee, O Israel!

O come, Desire of Nations; bind
All peoples in one
heart and mind, Bid envy, strife, and quarrels cease;
Fill the whole world with heaven's peace.
Rejoice! Rejoice! Emmanuel
Shall come to thee, O Israel!
Amen.
