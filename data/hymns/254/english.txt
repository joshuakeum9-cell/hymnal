# 254 I Hear Thy Welcome Voice: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed (Korean-English hymnal wording)

I hear Thy welcome voice
That calls me, Lord, to Thee
For cleansing in Thy precious blood
That flowed on Calvary.
I am coming, Lord;
Coming now to Thee:
Wash me, cleanse me, in the blood
That flowed on Calvary.

Though I come weak and vile,
Thou dost my strength assure;
Thou dost my vileness fully cleanse,
Till spotless all and pure.
I am coming, Lord;
Coming now to Thee:
Wash me, cleanse me, in the blood
That flowed on Calvary.

'Tis Jesus calls me on
To perfect faith and love,
To perfect hope, and peace, and trust,
For earth and heaven above.
I am coming, Lord;
Coming now to Thee:
Wash me, cleanse me, in the blood
That flowed on Calvary.

'Tis Jesus who confirms
The blessed work within,
By adding grace to welcomed grace,
Where reigned the power of sin.
I am coming, Lord;
Coming now to Thee:
Wash me, cleanse me, in the blood
That flowed on Calvary.

His blood the witness gives
Within my heart for me
O, Savior, heed my every pray'r,
My full abundance be!
I am coming, Lord;
Coming now to Thee:
Wash me, cleanse me, in the blood
That flowed on Calvary.
Amen.
