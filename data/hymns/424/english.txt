# 424 Take My Heart, O Father: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed

Take my heart, O Father!
Make and keep it all Thine own
Let Thy Spirit melt and break it
This proud heart of sin and stone.

Father, make me pure and lowly,
Fond of peace and free from strife
Turning from the paths unholy
Of this vain and sinful life.

Ever let Thy grace surround me,
Strengthen me with pow'r divine
Till Thy cords of love have bound me:
Make me to be wholly Thine.

May the blood of Jesus heal me,
And my sins be all forgiv'n
Holy Sporit, take and seal me,
Guide me in the path to heaven.
