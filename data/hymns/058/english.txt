# 58 Morning Hymns: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed

Morning hymns, O Lord, with praises
For the past night's gentle rest
Safe in Thee, the heart upraises
To Thy name, forever blest!

Health of body, peace of spirit!
Breath of life and hearts at ease!
Illness heal'd by Jesus' merit!
Praise we God for all of these.

As Thy love has shin'd around us
Through the watches of the night.
So, through earth's dark night surround us
With Thine own immortal light.

Hear, O spirit, our petition:
From this day our lives comtrol.
Bring Thy work to full fruition,
Make us perfect, make us whole! Amen.
