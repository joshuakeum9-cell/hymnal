# 446 I Need Thee Every Hour: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed

I need Thee every hour,
Most gracious Lord
No tender voice like
Thine Can peace afford
With Thee what joy I capture!
Every hour is rapture!
O bless me now, my Saviour,
I come to Thee.

I need Thee every hour,
Stay Thou near by
Temptations lose their power
When Thou art nigh.
With Thee what joy I capture!
Every hour is rapture!
O bless me now, my Saviour,
I come to Thee.

I need Thee every hour,
In joy or pain
Come quickly and abide,
Or life is vain.
With Thee what joy I capture!
Every hour is rapture!
O bless me now, my Saviour,
I come to Thee.

I need Thee every hour,
Teach me Thy will
And Thy rich promises
In me fulfill.
With Thee what joy I capture!
Every hour is rapture!
O bless me now, my Saviour,
I come to Thee.
