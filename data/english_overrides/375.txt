# 375 English as printed in the Korean-English hymnal, source: https://www.prayertents.com/hymns?nh=375
Jesus, Savior, pilot me,
Lost on life's uncharted sea.
Lead me, Lord, that I may know
In what way You'd have me go.
Lead me, Lord, that I may know
In what way You'd have me go.

Jesus, Savior, hear my call;
Nothing do I know at all.
Teach me, Lord, to learn from You
What it is You'd have me do.
Teach me, Lord, to learn from You
What it is You'd have me do.

Savior, help me! Nor forget
I am like an infant yet.
Weak and strengthless, here I plead,
Help me, Lord, supply my need.
Weak and strengthless, here I plead,
Help me, Lord, supply my need!

Comfort, Lord, my heart of grief;
Heal it's hurt with Your relief.
Helpless but for You I stray;
Comfort me and be my stay.
Helpless but for You I stray;
Comfort me and be my stay.
Amen.
