They that wait upon the Lord
Shall mount up, shall mount up,
On wings as eagles,
They shall rise above the strife,
Far beyond the cares of life,
Mounting up, mounting up,
On wings as eagles.
Then wait upon the Lord,
O wait upon the Lord;
Thy strength thou shalt renew;
O wait upon the Lord.

Waiting thus upon the Lord
They shall walk, they shall walk,
And shall not faint,
They shall scale the rugged steep
Searching for the wand'ring sheep,
They shall walk, they shall walk,
And shall not faint.
Then wait upon the Lord,
O wait upon the Lord;
Thy strength thou shalt renew;
O wait upon the Lord.

Waiting thus upon the Lord
They shall run, they shall run,
And not be weary,
They shall see the heav'nly prize
Waiting there beyond the skies,
They shall run, they shall run,
And not be weary.
Then wait upon the Lord,
O wait upon the Lord;
Thy strength thou shalt renew;
O wait upon the Lord.

They that wait upon the Lord
Shall mount up, shall mount up,
On wings as eagles,
They shall see the Saviour's face,
Revel in His boundless grace,
Mounting up, mounting up,
On wings as eagles.
Then wait upon the Lord,
O wait upon the Lord;
Thy strength thou shalt renew;
O wait upon the Lord.
