# 341 Jesus, I My Cross Have Taken: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed

Jesus, I my cross have taken,
All to leave, and flolow Thee
Destitute, despised,forsaken,
Thou, from hence, my all shalt be.
Perish every fond ambition,
All I've sought, and hoped, or known
Yet how rich is my condotion,
God and heaven are still my own.

Let the world despise sand leave me,
Saviour, too
Human hearts and look deceive me,
Thou art not, like man, untrue
And, while Thou shalt smile upon me,
God of wisdom, love, and, might,
Foes may hate, and friends may shun me:
Show Thy face, and all is bright.

Man may trouble and distess me,
'Twill but drive me to Thy breast
Life with trials hard may press me,
Heav'n will bring me sweeter rest
O 'tis not in grief to harm me
While Thy love is left to me
O 'twere not in joy to charm me:
Where that joy unmixed with Thee. Amen.
