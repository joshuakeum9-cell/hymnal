"In His steps" I follow as I go. On my pilgrim journey here below.
"In His steps" I follow day by day, Trusting Him to lead the way.
Gladly in His steps I follow, I follow, I follow.
Gladly in His steps I follow, Gladly in His steps I go.

"In His steps" what peace and joy I know. Every day my path doth brighter grow.
"In His steps" His spirit dwells within. Cleansing me from every sin.
Gladly in His steps I follow, I follow, I follow.
Gladly in His steps I follow, Gladly in His steps I go.

"In His steps" how sweet to walk with Him. E'en tho' clouds my pathway often "dim,
