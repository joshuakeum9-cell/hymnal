# 326 English as printed in the Korean-English hymnal, source: https://www.prayertents.com/hymns?nh=326
Serving the Lord, repentant
And sorry for my sin,
I'll give my love to Jesus
And gladly serve for Him.
In Christ, Wherever I be,
There whatever I do,
For Him Who died for me
Lord, I would be true!

Since Christ gave new life to me
According to His will,
Through life now would I truly
Serve for my Master still.
In Christ, Wherever I be,
There whatever I do,
For Him Who died for me
Lord, I would be true!

With all my heart and being
Would I obey God's word,
And, faith and life agreeing,
Serve ever for my Lord.
In Christ, Wherever I be,
There whatever I do,
For Him Who died for me
Lord, I would be true!

His power my strength and unction
His mandate to obey,
Would I live out my function
And serve throughout my day.
In Christ, Wherever I be,
There whatever I do,
For Him Who died for me
Lord, I would be true!

When, earthly service finished,
To heaven I ascent,
Through ages undiminished
I'll serve Him without end.
In Christ, Wherever I be,
There whatever I do,
For Him Who died for me
Lord, I would be true!
