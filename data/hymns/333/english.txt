# 333 Be Thou Faithful unto Death: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed (Korean-English hymnal wording)

Be thou faithful unto death,
True to Him Who gives us breath.
Grief or hardship cannot sever
Jesus' faithful love forever.
So be faithful to the last;
Live for Christ till life is past.

Be thou faithful unto death,
True to Him Who gives us breath.
Bright our crown is! Sweet His voice is,
His in whom our heart rejoices!
Be but faithful to the last;
Live for Christ till life is past.

Be thou faithful unto death,
True to Him Who gives us breath.
While we strive to serve our Savior,
Grant us, Lord, Your aid and favor
To be faithful to the last,
Live for Christ till life is past.
Amen.
