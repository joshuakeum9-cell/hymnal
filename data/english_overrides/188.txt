# 188 Come to Our Poor Nature's Night - George Rawson, source: https://www.prayertents.com/hymns?nh=188 , https://hymnary.org/text/come_to_our_poor_natures_night
Come to our poor nature's night
With the blessed inward light,
Holy Ghost the Infinite,
Comforter Divine.

We are sinful, cleanse us, Lord,
Sick and faint, Thy strength afford,
Lost, until by Thee restored,
Comforter Divine.

Like the dew Thy peace distil;
Guide, subdue our wayward will,
Things of Christ unfolding still,
Comforter Divine.

In us, for us, intercede,
And with voiceless groanings plead
Our unutterable need,
Comforter Divine.

In us "Abba, Father," cry,
Earnest of our bliss on high,
Seal of immortality,
Comforter Divine.

Search for us the depths of God!
Bear us up the starry road
To the height of Thine abode,
Comforter Divine.
