# 209 From Every Stormy Wind That Blows: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed (Korean-English hymnal wording)

From every stormy wind that blows,
From every swelling tide of woes,
There is a calm, a sure retreat:
'Tis found before the mercy seat.

There is a place where Jesus sheds
The oil of gladness on our heads;
A place than all besides more sweet:
It is the bloodbought mercy seat.

There is a scene where spirits blend,
Where friend holds fellow ship with friend,
Tho' sundered far, by faith they meet
Before one common mercy seat.

Oh, let my hand forget her skill,
My tongue be silent, cold and still,
This bounding heart forget to beat,
If I forget Thy mercy seat.
Amen.
