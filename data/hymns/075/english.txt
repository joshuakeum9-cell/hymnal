# 75 God, Be Merciful To Us: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed (Korean-English hymnal wording)

God, be merciful to us
In the greatness of Thy grace,
And to us who are but dust
Grant the joy to see Thy face.

By the worldwide message given,
Thou hast made salvation known,
And all peoples under heaven
Lift their praises to Thy throne.

Thou dost govern in holiness,
Truth in judgment dost employ;
While the nations Thou dost bless
Bless Thy name with holy joy.

All that comes to pass on earth,
Lord, Thy bounteous grace supplies;
And we laud Thy matchless worth
From all nations beneath the skies.

Blest of God Who names us His,
Raining bounties from above,
Earth and all that in it is
Fears the Lord with joy and love!
Amen.
