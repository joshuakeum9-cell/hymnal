# 148 On Calvary's Brow My Savior Died: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed

On Calvary's brow my Savior died,
'Twas there my Lord was crucified;
'Twas on the cross He bled for me
And purchased there my pardon free.
O Calvary! dark Calvary!
Where Jesus shed His blood for me
O Calvary! blest Calvary!
'Twas there my Savior died for me.

'Mid rending rocks and darkening skies
My Savior bows His head and dies;
The opening weil reveals the way
To heaven's joys and endless day.
O Calvary! dark Calvary!
Where Jesus shed His blood for me
O Calvary! blest Calvary!
'Twas there my Savior died for me.

Water and blood, when Jesus died,
Together flowed His pierced side;
They cleansed my guilt eternally,
Saved by His grace at Calvary!
O Calvary! dark Calvary!
Where Jesus shed His blood for me
O Calvary! blest Calvary!
'Twas there my Savior died for me.

This body, Lord, is all I own
Thy broken body to atone.
In shame and love I give it thee,
Who bought my life on Calvary!
O Calvary! dark Calvary!
Where Jesus shed His blood for me
O Calvary! blest Calvary!
'Twas there my Savior died for me.
