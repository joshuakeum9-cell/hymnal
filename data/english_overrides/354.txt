# 354 Mounting Up (They That Wait Upon the Lord) - Belle Staples, source: https://hymnary.org/hymn/SMU11908/2 (page scans of Songs of Mounting Up, 1908, no. 2, pp. 2-3)
They that wait up-on the Lord
Shall mount up, shall mount up,
On wings as ea-gles,
They shall rise a-bove the strife,
Far be-yond the cares of life,
Mount-ing up, mount-ing up,
On wings as ea-gles.
Then wait up-on the Lord,
O wait up-on the Lord;
Thy strength thou shalt re-new;
O wait up-on the Lord.

Wait-ing thus up-on the Lord
They shall walk, they shall walk,
And shall not faint,
They shall scale the rug-ged steep
Search-ing for the wand-'ring sheep,
They shall walk, they shall walk,
And shall not faint.
Then wait up-on the Lord,
O wait up-on the Lord;
Thy strength thou shalt re-new;
O wait up-on the Lord.

Wait-ing thus up-on the Lord
They shall run, they shall run,
And not be wea-ry,
They shall see the heav'nly prize
Wait-ing there be-yond the skies,
They shall run, they shall run,
And not be wea-ry.
Then wait up-on the Lord,
O wait up-on the Lord;
Thy strength thou shalt re-new;
O wait up-on the Lord.

They that wait up-on the Lord
Shall mount up, shall mount up,
On wings as ea-gles,
They shall see the Saviour's face,
Rev-el in His bound-less grace,
Mount-ing up, mount-ing up,
On wings as ea-gles.
Then wait up-on the Lord,
O wait up-on the Lord;
Thy strength thou shalt re-new;
O wait up-on the Lord.
