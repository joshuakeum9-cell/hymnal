# 566 A Little Child May Know: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed

A little child may know
Our Father's name of love
'Tis written on the earth below,
And on the sky above.

Around me when I look,
His handiwork I see
This world is like a picture book
To teach His love to me.

Lovely the meadow flow'rs
Waken'd by gentle rain
Lovely the rainbow in the show're,
Arching the heav'nly plain!

And in the nighttime, too,
All of those stars I see,
Shining so fair up in the blue,
Whisper God's love to me.
