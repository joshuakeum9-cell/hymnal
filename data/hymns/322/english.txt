# 322 Gather Us In, Thou Love: English words, one verse per block.
# status: proposed

Gather us in, Thou love that fillest all
Gather our rival faiths within Thy fold.
Rend each man's
temple veil and bid it fall,
That we, may know that Thou hast been of old

Gather us in: we worship only Thee
In varied names we stretch a common hand
In diverse forms a common soul we see
In many ships we seek one spirit land.

Each sees one colour of Thy rainbow light,
Each looks upon one tint and calls it heaven
Thou art the fullness of our partial sight
We are not perfect till we find the seven. Amen.
